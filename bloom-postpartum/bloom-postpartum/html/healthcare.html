SUPPORT STARTS WITH ONE STEP

Asking for help is a sign of care.

Whether you want to speak with a doctor, counselor, therapist, or another healthcare professional, support can begin with a simple conversation.

EXPLORE YOUR OPTIONS

Different kinds of support are available

The type of support you choose can depend on your needs, preferences, and what feels comfortable for you.

🩺

Doctor or Healthcare Provider

A doctor or healthcare provider can listen to your concerns, discuss your well-being, and guide you toward appropriate care or referrals.

💬

Counselor or Therapist

Mental-health professionals can provide a private space to talk about emotional challenges, stress, adjustment, and coping strategies.

🌿

Postpartum Specialist

Specialists with experience in postpartum care can help address challenges related to emotional well-being and adjustment after childbirth.

🤝

Community Support

Support groups and community services can help you feel connected with others and access local guidance and resources.

HOW TO BEGIN

Taking the first step can be simple.

You do not need to have all the answers before reaching out. You can simply describe what you have been experiencing and ask what support options are available.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

A conversation can be a starting point.

You don't need to prepare a perfect explanation before speaking with a healthcare professional.

You can share what has changed, what feels difficult, and any concerns you may have. The professional can help you understand possible next steps.

✓

Your concerns deserve to be taken seriously.

✓

You can ask questions about different support options.

✓

You can bring someone you trust for additional support.

✓

It is okay to seek a second opinion if needed.
